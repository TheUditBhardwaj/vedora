The Studio Philosophy

Made slowly.
Given wholeheartedly.

Vedora began with a simple thought: what if a bouquet could be more than a fleeting moment?

Each handmade bloom is shaped stem by stem, with room for color, personality, and the tiny details that make a gift truly personal. Using plush chenille wire, we form velvety petals that hold their shape indefinitely. No wilting, no dropped petals, and no goodbye.
