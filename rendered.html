The Studio Philosophy

Made slowly. Given wholeheartedly.

Vedora began with a simple thought: what if a bouquet could be more than a beautiful moment? Each handmade bloom is shaped stem by stem, with room for colour, personality, and the little details that make a gift feel personal.

Using plush chenille pipe cleaners, we form velvety petals that hold their shape indefinitely. No drying out, no dropped petals, and no fleeting goodbye.
